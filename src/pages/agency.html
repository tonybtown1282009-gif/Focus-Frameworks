FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does a site really take?

Starter Sites typically launch in 7–10 days. Business Pro builds are 14–21 days from kickoff. We give you a firm timeline after the discovery call so you can plan around it.

Do I own the site after launch?

Yes — completely. Code, content, and domain are yours. We hand you the keys and you can take the site anywhere, anytime. No lock-in, ever.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Absolutely. We hand off clear instructions for the common stuff (text, images, hours). For bigger changes, our Monthly Care Plan covers up to 2 hours of updates per month — or you can hire us per-project.

What about hosting?

You can host anywhere. We recommend (and use) Netlify — it's fast, secure, and often free for small business traffic. Monthly Care Plan customers get hosting handled for them.

Do you do logos and branding too?

We can. Simple logo refreshes are included in Business Pro on request. For full brand identity work, we partner with a small handful of local designers we trust and price it separately.

What if I already have a site I want updated?

Common situation. Send us the link in the form below and we'll tell you honestly whether a refresh or a rebuild makes more sense. Sometimes a rebuild is cheaper in the long run — we'll show you the math.

Do you work outside NE Ohio?

Yes, occasionally — but our roots and most of our clients are local. Local clients get priority scheduling and in-person meetings when useful.
